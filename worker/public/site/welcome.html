    The details, if you want them

    What decides what gets hidden?
    • Your own calls outrank everything, including us.
    • The AI reply bot list on X: levelsio's list, Clankwall and our reviewed list. Accounts levelsio blocked silently and never reviewed stay visible unless you switch them on in the popup, because some may be real people.
    • The platform's own AI label, where there is one: YouTube's, and X's label on AI images and video.
    • The account's pattern. Accounts that label nearly every upload as AI are slop farms; ordinary ones label none.
    • The writing itself, only if you switch the writing check on. It is off until you do, because it is the only part that sends a post's words away. See the privacy policy.
    What leaves my browser?

    The bot list is downloaded whole, with no identifier, and matched on your computer. Other lookups send only the first four characters of a hash. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold. The privacy policy lists everything that is sent.

    How do my reports work?

    Pressing AI SLOP hides a post for you at once and adds it to a queue a person reviews by hand. On X, the link to the reply also goes to levelsio's report queue (nothing about you, just the link), where his AI and then he check it before it joins his list. One person cannot bury anyone. Got one wrong? Not slop is just as useful. To keep your calls to yourself, turn off Contribute reports in the popup.
