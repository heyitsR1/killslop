Uninstalled

KillSlop is gone from your browser.

Everything it stored on your device went with it. If you have ten seconds, tell us what went wrong. It is the only way we find out, and it is anonymous unless you choose otherwise.

What made you remove it?

  • Read by a person, never published, never sold.
  • No account, no tracking, and no identifier is attached to this page.
  • The only thing the link carries is which version you had. See the privacy policy.

If it was fixable

Most of it is a setting.

If it hid too much, Dim it rather than Hide it, or turn off opinion votes and keep only the channels measured from YouTube's own AI labels. If it missed too much, the writing check reads posts that X and LinkedIn never label.
