1.4K AI SLOP
Marked as AI slop. Hidden for you. Hide whole channel Undo

AI slop is filling YouTube. Generated documentaries, AI voice-overs on stock footage, synthetic music and animated kids' content, uploaded faster than anyone could watch it. YouTube labels some of it, then recommends it anyway.

What it does

Your feed, minus the slop.

Three independent signals decide what gets hidden, and your own clicks outrank all of them.

Reads YouTube's own label

Creators must disclose realistic AI content, and YouTube now labels some of it itself. KillSlop reads that label on each video: about 1 KB per check, once per video, ever.

Catches whole channels

Slop farms label nearly every upload; real channels label none. When at least 60% of five or more sampled uploads carry the label, the channel is hidden.

Checks an open, reviewed list

Every entry says why it is there: measured, voted or reviewed. While the list is young, a person checks each entry before it is served to anyone.

One click, and yours wins

AI SLOP sits beside like and dislike, and in the Shorts bar. Your mark hides a video for you at once, and your Not slop outranks everything else.

Always says why

A hidden video leaves a thin note with the reason, a Show button and a Not slop button. Nothing disappears without a trace.

Hide it or dim it

Remove slop from the page, or grey it out so you can still see what was caught. One switch pauses everything.

How it decides

Five checks, cheapest first.

The first check that answers wins. Your own word comes first, ahead of YouTube and the community alike.

Tier Check Cost What it means
00 Your mark free Absolute. Your Not slop outranks YouTube and the community.
01 Local cache free Verdicts never expire, because a disclosure never changes.
02 Channel inference free At least 60% of at least 5 sampled uploads labelled AI, and the whole channel is hidden.
03 Community list 1 request / bucket Batched, private by design, and every entry is tagged with its evidence.
04 Label checkInnerTube probe ~1 KB / video Throttled, in the background, once per video ever.

The open list

An open list of slop that anyone can use.

Every channel the list has decided is published in the clear under CC BY-SA 4.0. Blocklists, researchers and other filters can use it without running KillSlop, as long as they credit KillSlop and share what they build from it under the same licence.

disclosure
Measured. Other KillSlop users saw the channel's own uploads carry YouTube's AI label.
vote
Opinion. People pressed AI SLOP or Not slop. You can switch votes off and keep measurement only.
review
Checked by hand. A maintainer looked at it, and that call wins in every mode.

Privacy

Built not to know what you watch.

No account, no ads, no analytics. The few things that leave your browser are listed here, and in full in the privacy policy.

sha256(id) → 4 characters

Lookups by hash prefix

To check a video, the extension sends the first 4 characters of a hash of its id. The server answers with the whole bucket, one of 65,536, and the match happens in your browser. The list never learns which video you asked about.

vote · measurement · feedback

Only what you choose to send

A mark sends the id you marked. A channel measurement sends a channel id and its counts, once. Feedback sends what you typed. Switch off Contribute reports and your marks stay on your device.

sha256(value + entry + salt)

Salted and unlinkable

A random install id and your network are stored only as salted hashes, scoped to the one entry you voted on. Enough to count you once, never enough to link two of your votes.

credentials: 'omit'

No cookies, no account

Label checks go to YouTube without your cookies, so they are never tied to your YouTube account. KillSlop has no sign-in, and no way to know who you are.

Honest limits

It will not catch everything.

A filter that only reads the label misses most slop. Channel measurement, the open list and human review exist to close that gap, and some will still get through. When KillSlop gets one wrong, one click puts it back.
