# Code of conduct

KillSlop labels content, not people. Hold every discussion here to the same
standard.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with the person
  who holds them.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, including against the creators
  of channels discussed here. "This channel posts AI slop" is a claim to back
  with evidence. It is never licence to abuse the people behind the channel.
- No doxxing. Never post anyone's personal information, and never try to
  work out who voted for or reported something.
- Keep reports factual: link the content and say what you observed.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To raise a conduct problem privately, use the extension's **Send feedback**
page and leave an email if you want a reply.
